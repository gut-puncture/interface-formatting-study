\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Format sensitivity.} Changing only the format of a prompt, such as separators, casing, Markdown or JSON, can move accuracy by tens of points~\citep{sclar2024quantifying,he2024does}, and a single prompt template gives unstable rankings of models~\citep{mizrahi2024state}. Closest to us, \citet{mehta2026format} wrap benchmark questions in five wrapper families and find that much of the spread comes from replies that break the requested format. \citet{liu2026understanding} show that templates move a model's output probabilities more than the questions themselves. These studies measure accuracy. We separate replies that break the format from replies that lose the answer, use only answers the model knows, and locate where in the network the loss happens.

\paragraph{Distraction and long inputs.} One irrelevant sentence lowers accuracy on grade-school math~\citep{shi2023large}, and related but irrelevant passages mislead models~\citep{wu2024how}. Accuracy falls as inputs grow long~\citep{levy2024same,hsieh2024ruler}, even when the extra tokens are whitespace and retrieval is perfect~\citep{du2025context}, and depends on where the relevant text sits~\citep{liu2024lost}. Tool-using agents degrade under noisy tool outputs~\citep{wang2026agentnoisebench}. We keep everything after the question fixed, vary the amount of text before it in three steps, and compare interface formats with plain prose of the same length.

\paragraph{Locating computation.} Activation patching copies internal states from one run into another to find which states carry a behaviour~\citep{vig2020causal,meng2022locating,wang2023interpretability}; its results depend on the metric and on the source of the copied states~\citep{zhang2024towards,heimersheim2024how}. In factual recall, attention moves information from the subject's tokens to the last position in the middle layers~\citep{geva2023dissecting}. With misleading demonstrations, answers diverge at a critical layer~\citep{halawi2024overthinking}. Models also hold knowledge they do not state~\citep{orgad2025llms,gekhman2025inside}. We use patching with a matched control: the same states from a different question.
